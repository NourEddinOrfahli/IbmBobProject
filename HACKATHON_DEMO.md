# Three-Minute Hackathon Demo

1. **0:00–0:30 — The problem:** Open Al-Tariq in Arabic and explain that
   NASA's raw space data is valuable but difficult for Arabic-speaking
   non-specialists to understand.
2. **0:30–1:15 — Daily signal:** Show the dashboard's NASA APOD story, source
   attribution, scientific explanation, key facts, and space-weather panel.
3. **1:15–2:10 — Interpretation:** Upload a NASA image in
   **المترجم الفضائي**, ask a focused Arabic question, and show the structured
   observations, confidence, and distinction between visible evidence and
   scientific interpretation.
4. **2:10–2:40 — Conversation:** Move the image context into the Arabic AI
   chat and ask a follow-up question. Highlight that credentials stay on the
   backend and responses are validated.
5. **2:40–3:00 — Product close:** Open قصص الكون, save a story, and explain that
   the platform turns live NASA data into an accessible Arabic learning path.

For a reliable demo, configure both `NASA_API_KEY` and `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`
before starting and keep one known NASA image ready locally.
